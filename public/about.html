About the product

Useful SEO planning, without the busywork.

30SecSEO is a free web-based SEO planning tool built to make the early stages of content optimization easier to understand and faster to complete.

Why 30SecSEO exists

SEO work often becomes a collection of small tasks: choosing a search topic, drafting a title, writing a meta description, planning headings, collecting related questions, checking internal links and remembering structured data. For a solo creator, moving between tools for each task can make a simple content project feel unnecessarily complicated.

30SecSEO brings those planning steps into one workflow. You enter a keyword, choose an output language and content type, and can optionally describe your audience, tone, target length or a competitor domain. The generated blueprint is meant to give you a practical starting point that you can inspect and edit.

What makes the workflow different

The product focuses on outputs that can be reviewed individually. Instead of treating an AI response as a finished article, the interface separates titles, metadata, keyword ideas, outlines, image alt text, snippet ideas, schema, social tags and a publishing checklist.

Important: 30SecSEO does not promise a particular Google ranking. Search performance depends on relevance, competition, content quality, links, technical health, user needs and changes to search systems.

Who can use it

  • Bloggers planning new articles.
  • Small businesses preparing helpful resource pages.
  • Freelance writers building content briefs.
  • SEO practitioners who want a fast first-pass outline.
  • Creators working in languages supported by the output selector.

Our approach to AI output

AI can accelerate drafting, but it can also make mistakes. We encourage users to check facts, remove generic wording, add original examples and experience, and make sure the final page genuinely helps the reader. That human review is part of the workflow rather than an optional afterthought.
